Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Solo Entrepreneur ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website or purchase our products.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Payment information: When you make a purchase, Razorpay processes your payment. We receive your name, email, and phone number. We do not store card or bank details.
  • Order data: Product purchased, amount paid, and payment ID stored in our database for order fulfillment and account access.
  • Usage data: Basic analytics such as pages visited (no personal tracking).

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To fulfill your orders and provide access to purchased products.
  • To send transactional emails related to your purchases.
  • To occasionally send tips and updates from Prithal (you can unsubscribe anytime).

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers. Payments are processed by Razorpay and governed by their Privacy Policy. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for authentication (login sessions). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing prithal@thesoloentrepreneur.in.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, email: prithal@thesoloentrepreneur.in
