BUILD AN AI VIDEO
CONTENT STUDIO
IN INDIA

Veo 3, ElevenLabs, and a laptop. No crew, no cameras, no budget. Just AI-generated stories that people actually watch.

✍️ Prithal Bhardwaj📅 May 2025⏱ 7 min read🇮🇳 India-specific
💡 Quick Summary

An AI video content studio using tools like Google Veo 3 costs almost nothing to start. Year 1 revenue: $4,500–$6,000 (~₹3.7–5L) from ads and digital products. Year 3 potential: $150,000+ (~₹1.25 crore). No camera, no crew — just prompts, editing, and a posting schedule.

Why This Is the Wildest Opportunity of 2025

Let me paint you a picture. A few months ago, creating a cinematic sci-fi video — neon cities, fog-covered streets, slow tracking shots — would cost you ₹5–10 lakh in production. Crew, cameras, VFX artists, post-production. The works.

Today? Google Veo 3 can generate that exact scene from a text prompt in minutes. And I mean genuinely cinematic — photorealistic, with camera movement, with lighting, with mood. The kind of content that would blow up on Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts.

The Indian creator economy is massive — 2+ billion short-form video viewers globally, and Indian creators are chronically underrepresented in the AI content space. That's your gap. Get in now, before everyone figures this out.

What Kind of Content Actually Works

Not all AI video formats go viral. You need a "scripted soul" — a concept with emotional resonance, not just pretty visuals. Here are the formats that consistently perform:

  • Cinematic world-building: "Places that could have been" — imagine a flooded Mumbai, a neon Delhi in 2150, ancient ruins of a future civilization. Mysterious, shareable, emotional.
  • Character vlogs: A fictional medieval blacksmith. An astronaut stuck on a moon colony. A robot navigating modern India. People fall in love with recurring characters.
  • Historical/Sci-fi reenactments: What if the Mughal Empire had steam power? What if the Indus Valley civilization built skyscrapers? History buffs plus AI nerds = explosive niche.
  • Montage series: "The Fall of Humanity," "Earth 3.0," "Cities Lost to the Sea." Poetic titles, ambient music, striking visuals. These rack up saves and shares.

Pick one. Stick to it for 30 days. Find your audience before you diversify.

Building Your MVP in 1 Week

Here's my honest approach to getting started without overthinking it:

Day 1–2: Pick your format and write 5 prompts

Don't jump straight to generating videos. Spend time on the concept. Write 5 detailed video prompts specifying style (cinematic 35mm, surreal, vintage), content, camera movement (dolly zoom, slow pan, orbit shot), and lighting. Example: "Cinematic 35mm film of a crumbling neon-lit megacity at dusk, slow tracking shot, fog, abandoned streets, Blade Runner aesthetic. No people."

Day 3–4: Generate and edit

Feed your prompts into Veo 3 (via Google Gemini) and RunwayML. Render 5–10 clips. Bring them into CapCut. Add atmospheric music from Soundraw. If you're doing voiceover, set up your ElevenLabs voice. Keep clips 30–60 seconds for Reels/Shorts.

Day 5–7: Post and analyze

Post all 5 videos across Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok. Use strong hooks in captions: "Would you visit this lost city?", "A glimpse into Earth 3.0", "What Mumbai looks like in 2150." Track which gets the most saves and shares — that's your signal for what to double down on.

Growing the Channel

A few things that accelerate growth significantly:

  • Post consistently: 3–5 times per week minimum. The algorithm rewards consistency more than quality at first. You're building a habit loop for your audience.
  • Engage in comments: Reply to every comment in character if you're doing a character-based channel. "As the blacksmith, I appreciate your kind words, traveller." Fans love this.
  • Use polls and questions: "What happens next — does the astronaut escape or stay?" Community-driven storytelling spikes engagement and saves.
  • Cross-post everything: Same video, TikTok + Reels + Shorts. It takes 5 extra minutes and triples your reach.
  • Pitch to AI newsletters and tech blogs: "Indian creator builds entire film studio using only AI" is a genuinely interesting story. Many publications will cover it for free.

What Will Kill Your Channel Early

I've seen this pattern too many times:

  • Posting without a hook: The first 2 seconds of your Reel are everything. If you don't hook them immediately, they scroll. Write the hook first, build the video around it.
  • No character consistency: Generic AI clips are a dime a dozen. The channels that build audiences have recurring characters, recurring aesthetics, recurring vibes. Pick yours and stick to it.
  • Waiting for perfection: Post imperfect work. Veo 3 will glitch sometimes. That's fine. Ship it, learn what works, iterate.
  • Skipping the digital product: Don't wait until you have 10,000 followers to sell a prompt pack. Even 500 followers can generate ₹20,000–50,000 if they're the right audience.

Your 5-Day Action Plan

Action Plan
  • Day 1: Choose your content format (cinematic worlds, character vlogs, historical reenactments). Study 3 successful AI video accounts on Instagram/TikTok.
  • Day 2: Write 5 detailed video prompts. Sign up for Veo 3 (via Gemini), RunwayML free plan, and CapCut.
  • Day 3: Generate your first 5 clips. Edit them in CapCut with atmospheric music from Soundraw. Add text overlays and hooks.
  • Day 4: Create accounts on Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok. Post first video with a strong hook caption. Set up a Gumroad page for your future prompt pack.
  • Day 5: Post video 2, reply to every comment, analyze which video did better and why. Start writing your prompt pack — 20 prompts, ₹499 price point.

The bar for AI video content in India is still incredibly low. Most Indian creators aren't doing this yet. That means the first ones who do — and who do it consistently — will capture enormous organic reach before everyone else catches up. This window won't stay open forever.
