START AN AI ADS AGENCY IN INDIA WITH ZERO INVESTMENT

Traditional studios charge ₹50K–₹2L per ad. Here's how to undercut them by 90% using AI — and still deliver better work.

✍️ Prithal Bhardwaj📅 May 2025⏱ 7 min read🇮🇳 India-specific
💡 Quick Summary

Traditional agencies charge ₹50K–₹2L per video ad. Using AI tools like VEO3, Pika, and Runway, you can deliver comparable quality at ₹2,999–₹14,999 per project — with zero crew, zero studio, and zero upfront investment. Year-1 revenue potential: ₹4.95 lakh.

The Opportunity Nobody Talks About

Every small business in India needs video ads. The Shopify store owner in Surat, the coaching institute in Pune, the D2C brand in Bengaluru — they all need high-quality 30-second commercials. But here's the thing: most of them can't afford the ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000 that traditional production houses charge. So they either skip ads entirely or settle for garbage-quality DIY videos.

That gap is your business. The global video ad market is crossing $150 billion by 2027. In India, digital ad spend is exploding — especially on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. And right now, AI tools have made it possible for one person with a laptop to produce studio-level commercials. No cameras. No crew. No editing team. Just you, your prompts, and a few free tools.

I'll be honest — this isn't some overnight thing. But it's one of the most realistic zero-investment businesses you can start this week if you're willing to put in a month of learning.

You don't need Premiere Pro. You don't need a DaVinci Resolve license. Here's the actual stack, most of it free to start:

VEO3 (Google's video model) is genuinely impressive right now. Pika is great for product-style ads with motion effects. Runway is solid for cinematic transitions. The trick is learning JSON prompting — structuring your prompts in a specific way to get consistent, high-quality outputs. That's the real skill you're selling, not just "using AI."

Your Service Packages

Pricing is where most people mess up. Don't charge too cheap (you'll attract nightmare clients) and don't charge too high when you're starting out. Here's a clean structure that works:

PackageWhat's IncludedPrice
Starter Ad1 AI-generated commercial (15–30 sec)₹2,999
Standard Ad1 ad + scriptwriting + 2 revisions₹5,999
Full Funnel Pack3 ad variations + headlines + copy + script₹14,999
White LabelBundle of 5 ads/month for agencies₹24,999/mo

The white-label package is where the real money is. Other agencies — even big ones — will happily outsource their video ad production to you if you're reliable and fast. LinkedIn DMs to agency owners in Mumbai and Hyderabad work surprisingly well for this.

Who Will Actually Pay You

The best clients for an AI ads agency in India are not the Fortune 500 companies. They already have in-house teams. Your sweet spot is:

  • Shopify / D2C brands — They're running Facebook and Instagram ads constantly and always need fresh creatives. A brand doing ₹5–20 lakh monthly revenue will happily pay ₹6K for an ad that converts.
  • Coaches and course creators — Every business coach on LinkedIn needs a 30-second "about me" video. Most have no idea how to make one. This is easy money.
  • SaaS startups — MVP explainers and product demo videos. Find them on Product Hunt India and LinkedIn.
  • YouTubers and creators — Sponsor segments, intros, ad creatives. DM creators with 10K–100K subscribers on YouTube directly.
  • Local businesses going digital — A restaurant in Bengaluru, a gym in Delhi, a boutique in Jaipur. These people have money and zero technical knowledge.

WhatsApp is genuinely underrated for B2B sales in India. Once you have a sample video, send it via WhatsApp to local business owners. The conversion rate on a visual product like video ads is much higher than cold email.

Getting Your First 5 Clients (No Ad Spend)

Here's what actually works when you're starting from zero:

The free ad challenge: Post on LinkedIn or Twitter — "I'll make you a free AI video ad if you comment below." Do this for the first 5 people. Post each result publicly. This one move can get you your first testimonials and potentially a viral post.

Fiverr and Upwork: Position yourself as an "AI Ad Expert." The platform already has buyers searching for affordable video ads. Your turnaround time of 24–48 hours (vs. a studio's 2–3 weeks) is a massive differentiator. Put it in your listing headline.

Cold DMs with a demo: Identify 20 small D2C brands or coaches daily. Create a 15-second sample ad for their product using their logo and product images. Send it cold. "Hey, made this quick demo for your brand — here's how a full version could look." This approach converts at 5–10% easily.

Reddit and Facebook groups: Share your prompt frameworks and process in groups like Indian Startups, D2C India, and Digital Marketing India communities. Give value first, mention your service second.

A single agency client at ₹24,999/month is 8x better than scrambling for 8 individual ₹2,999 orders.

The Honest Risks

AI video tools change fast. Free credits dry up. Models that work great today may get paywalled or replaced. The mitigation is simple: start building a paying client base before you rely on free tiers. Once you have 5 paying clients, you can afford a ₹3,000–5,000/month tool subscription and still be massively profitable.

The other real risk is quality consistency. AI-generated ads can look generic if you don't put in the work on prompting and post-production polish. Learn JSON prompting deeply in week 1. Watch tutorials on Runway's motion controls. The polish is what separates a ₹2,999 ad from a ₹14,999 one — and it's mostly editing and structure, not the raw AI output.

This Week's Action Plan

Your 7-Day Starter Plan
  1. Day 1–2: Sign up for VEO3, Pika, and ElevenLabs free tiers. Watch 3 tutorials on JSON video prompting.
  2. Day 3–4: Create 3 demo ads for 3 different niches (D2C, coaching, local biz). Upload to a Notion portfolio.
  3. Day 5: Set up a Carrd landing page with your packages and a Razorpay payment link.
  4. Day 6: Post the "free ad challenge" on LinkedIn and Twitter.
  5. Day 7: Send 20 cold DMs to D2C brands or coaches with a free demo of their product.

The global video ad market doesn't care whether your ad was shot with a ₹5 crore camera or generated by an AI in 20 minutes. Clients care about whether it converts. That's the edge you're selling — results, not process.
