Build an AI Domain Name Generator SaaS for Indian Startups

Affiliate commissions from GoDaddy, Hostinger & Namecheap = revenue without charging users. 20K users in Year 1 → ₹10 lakhs. Here's the full plan.

✍️ Prithal Bhardwaj📅 May 2025⏱ 9 min read🇮🇳 India-specific
💡 Quick Summary

NameWise AI is an AI-powered domain name generator that also compares prices across registrars in real time. Primary revenue: affiliate commissions of ₹50–₹500 per domain purchase. Year 1 target: 20,000 users, 4,000 purchases, ₹10 lakh revenue. Year 3 target: ₹1.05 crore.

What This Product Is

Every startup needs a domain name. Finding a good one is genuinely painful — you come up with something creative, check it on GoDaddy, it's taken, then you start adding random words or numbers until you settle for something you don't love. It's a terrible experience.

NameWise AI fixes this. The user types in their business idea, industry, or a few keywords. The AI generates 20–30 unique, brandable domain name suggestions — and instantly shows the price for each one across GoDaddy, Hostinger, Namecheap, and BigRock in INR. One click takes them to purchase. You earn a commission.

Simple. Free for users. Revenue from affiliate commissions on every domain sold.

Why India, Why Now

India's startup ecosystem is minting new companies every day. Every founder, every freelancer, every small business going online needs a domain. The existing tools are either very basic (GoDaddy's own generator is terrible) or not India-focused — they show prices in USD, don't show BigRock or Hostinger India options, and have clunky interfaces.

There's a specific gap for an Indian product that speaks the language — shows prices in INR, includes Indian registrars, understands Indian business naming conventions (we love names that end in ".in" or ".co.in", we like Hindi-English hybrid names, etc.).

The Indian SaaS market is booming and the startup ecosystem is creating new demand every week. This isn't a saturated market for an India-first tool.

What to Build

The MVP can be lean. Don't over-engineer it. Here's the core feature set:

  • AI name generation — User inputs keywords/industry, GPT generates unique brandable names. Focus on .com, .in, .co.in, .io, .ai extensions
  • Real-time price comparison — Pull live pricing from registrar APIs. Show registration and renewal prices side-by-side in INR
  • Social media handle checker — Instantly check if the Instagram, Twitter/X, and LinkedIn handles are also available. This is a huge value-add that competitors don't do well
  • Affiliate redirect — Every "Buy" button uses your affiliate link. This is your entire revenue model
  • User dashboard — Save favorite names, track what you've checked, compare shortlisted options

Future additions: basic logo suggestions using AI image gen, trademark conflict warnings (check if the name is already trademarked in India), and brand score rating based on pronounceability and memorability.

How to Build It

You don't need to be a developer to get version 1 out. Here's a practical tech path:

  • No-code/low-code MVP: Use Bubble or a React template + OpenAI API for name generation. Affiliate links are just static URLs — no complex integration needed at first.
  • Price comparison: Most registrars have affiliate dashboards with API access. Namecheap, BigRock, Hostinger all do. Pull pricing via their APIs or scrape it (check their ToS first).
  • Hosting: Deploy on Vercel or Railway. Very cheap to start.
  • Total dev cost: ₹50,000–₹1,50,000 if you hire a freelancer, or much less if you build it yourself with no-code tools.
📌 The key technical challenge

Real-time price comparison across multiple registrars is the hardest part to build reliably. Prices change. APIs rate-limit you. Consider caching prices with a 6-hour refresh cycle to start — it doesn't have to be perfectly real-time to be useful.

Getting Your First 20,000 Users

SEO is your best long-term channel. Rank for "domain name generator India", "best domain name ideas for startup", "cheap .in domain registration". These are searches with clear buying intent.

Short-term tactics:

  • Product Hunt launch — Indian founders are very active there. A good launch can get you 500–2,000 signups in a day.
  • IndieHackers and Hacker News — Share your tool's story. Builders love tools for builders.
  • Startup WhatsApp groups — There are hundreds of Indian startup WhatsApp communities. Share genuinely, not spammy.
  • Reddit India subreddits — r/indianstartups, r/IndiaInvestments, r/startups — post about your product's launch.
  • LinkedIn content — Post about the problem: "How I spent 3 hours trying to find a domain name for my startup" → here's the tool I built to solve it.

Start This Week

  1. Day 1: Sign up for affiliate programs — GoDaddy, Namecheap, Hostinger India, BigRock. Get your affiliate links ready.
  2. Day 2: Build a simple landing page with an email capture: "I'm building an AI domain name generator for Indian startups — join the waitlist."
  3. Day 3–4: Build MVP — even a simple Google Sheet where users enter keywords and you manually run GPT to give suggestions is enough to validate the concept.
  4. Day 5: Share in 5 startup communities. Get your first 50 beta users.
  5. Day 6–7: Collect feedback. Which names do they like? Do they click through to buy? Fix what's broken.
