WhatsApp AI Agent — Build It Free

Your number. Your knowledge base. Your AI. No credit card. No code.

If you run any kind of business in India, WhatsApp is where your customers are. Most D2C founders, coaches, consultants, and freelancers I know spend hours every week answering the same 10 questions on WhatsApp — pricing, availability, how to order, what's included.

A WhatsApp AI Agent fixes that. It answers for you, 24/7, from a knowledge base you define. And the best part: you can build one for free, with zero code, in under 10 minutes — using YCloud. This is the complete, step-by-step guide.

⚡ What You're Building

An AI agent connected to your WhatsApp Business number that automatically responds to incoming messages using your business information — product details, pricing, FAQs, policies. When someone needs a human, it hands off to you.

What You Need Before You Start

You need three things — all free. Think of them like Russian nesting dolls: each one sits inside the next.

1
Meta Business Manager
The master dashboard at business.facebook.com. Everything lives inside here.
2
Facebook Business Page
A Facebook Page for your business. It goes inside your Meta Business Manager.
3
WhatsApp Business Account
Your WhatsApp Business number. Connected to the Page. This is what YCloud plugs into.
🎯 Analogy

Meta Business Manager = your office building. Facebook Page = your company's reception desk. WhatsApp Business Account = the phone at the desk that your AI answers.

  • Use a personal Facebook account you actually own (Meta will verify it)
  • Your phone number must be active on WhatsApp Business App before you connect it
  • A real business email address is recommended (not Gmail)

Step 1 — Set Up Meta Business Manager

Go to business.facebook.com and create your business account. This is free and takes 5 minutes.

1
Go to business.facebook.com

Click Create account. Sign in with your personal Facebook account — Meta uses this to verify it's a real human.

2
Enter your business details

Business name, your name, and a business email. Use the name your customers know you by.

3
Verify your email

Meta sends a confirmation email. Click the link. Your Business Manager is now active.

4
Add your business info

Go to Business Settings → Business Info. Add your address, phone number, and website. This helps with Meta's verification process later.

Business Settings › Business Info
5
You're done with Meta BM

Keep this tab open — you'll come back to it. Now create your Facebook Business Page.

Step 2 — Create a Facebook Business Page

Your Facebook Page is the public face of your business on Meta's platform. Even if you never post on Facebook, you need it because WhatsApp Business accounts are linked to Pages.

1
Go to facebook.com/pages/create

Or go to Meta Business Manager → Pages → Add → Create a new Page.

2
Choose "Business or Brand"

Enter your Page name (your business name) and select a category. Pick something general like "Product/Service" or "Entrepreneur" if unsure.

3
Add a profile photo and cover

Use your logo or a clean photo. A complete Page looks more professional and helps with Meta's verification.

4
Add the Page to your Business Manager

In Meta Business Manager → Pages → Add → Add a Page you own. Select the Page you just created.

Business Manager › Pages › Add › Add a Page
5
Page is now inside your BM

Next: connect your WhatsApp Business number.

Step 3 — Connect Your WhatsApp Business Number

This is the step most people get confused about. There are two ways to use WhatsApp with Meta's API. You want Coexistence mode — it lets you keep using your regular WhatsApp Business App while also connecting to YCloud.

How to Connect Your Number via YCloud (Coexistence)

1
Create a free YCloud account

Go to ycloud.com and sign up. No credit card required.

2
Go to WhatsApp Accounts → Add Number

In the YCloud dashboard, find the WhatsApp Accounts section and click Add WhatsApp Business Number.

YCloud Dashboard › WhatsApp Accounts › Add Number
3
Choose "Connect via WhatsApp Business App (Coexistence)"

YCloud will ask you to scan a QR code with your WhatsApp Business App. Open WhatsApp Business → Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device → scan the QR.

Requirement: WhatsApp Business App version 2.24.17 or above. Update from Play Store / App Store if needed.
4
Connect to Meta Business Manager

YCloud will prompt you to log in to Meta and authorise the connection. Select your Business Manager account and Facebook Page.

5
Your number is now connected

YCloud shows your number as Active. Your existing WhatsApp Business App still works. You now have the API layer on top for AI features.

Step 4 — Build Your AI Agent in YCloud

With your WhatsApp number connected, here's how to build the actual AI agent. This takes about 10 minutes.

1
Create your AI Agent Profile

Go to AI Agent → Create Agent. Give it a name (e.g. "Priya from The Solo Entrepreneur"), a role, and a personality. This is what the AI "becomes" in your WhatsApp conversations.

AI Agent › Create Agent
Tip: Give it a human name. "Hi, I'm Priya from [Your Brand]" converts better than "Hi, I'm an AI Bot".
2
Build your Knowledge Base

This is what the AI knows and answers from. Add:

  • Your product/service descriptions and pricing
  • FAQs you answer most often
  • Shipping, return, or cancellation policies
  • How to buy / book / enrol
  • Your contact details and working hours
AI Agent › Knowledge Base › Add Content
3
Set the Assignment Rules — critical step

Go to WhatsApp Accounts → Number Settings → Assignment. Set it exactly like this:

Priority 1 — Assign to owner → OFF
Priority 2 — Assign to last handling agent → OFF
Priority 3 — Assign to: AI Agent → Your agent name → ACTIVE ✓
🚨 Turn OFF Priority 2. If you've chatted with someone before, Priority 2 will route messages to you instead of the AI. Keep ONLY Priority 3 active.
4
Test with a fresh number

Ask someone who has never messaged your WhatsApp Business number before to send a message. The AI Agent should reply automatically within seconds.

Why "fresh"? Saved contacts may have existing conversation threads that trigger Priority 2. Always test with a number that has never contacted you.
5
Set up human handoff

In AI Agent → Skills, add a handoff skill so the agent transfers to you when someone says things like "talk to a human" or "speak to [your name]".

AI Agent › Skills › Add Handoff Action

Go-Live Checklist

  • WhatsApp Business App number connected to YCloud
  • AI Agent Profile configured — name, role, personality set
  • Knowledge Base populated with your business info
  • Assignment Rules: Priority 1 & 2 OFF, Priority 3 → AI Agent ACTIVE
  • Tested with a fresh number — AI responds correctly
  • Tested 3–4 different questions from your knowledge base
  • Tested an off-topic question — AI handles gracefully
  • Human handoff skill configured and tested

Common Issues & Fixes

❌ AI not responding?

Priority 2 is likely ON and routing to you. Go to Assignment Rules → turn Priority 2 OFF.

❌ Saved contacts not getting AI?

Go to their conversation in the Inbox → Resolve it → ask them to message again fresh.

❌ Agent giving wrong answers?

Knowledge base needs more detail. Add the specific Q&A it got wrong and re-test.

❌ QR code not scanning?

Update WhatsApp Business App to version 2.24.17 or above from Play Store / App Store.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use my existing WhatsApp Business number for a YCloud AI Agent?
Yes. YCloud supports Coexistence mode, which lets you connect your existing WhatsApp Business App number to YCloud without porting it or losing your chat history. You keep using your regular number — YCloud adds an AI agent layer on top of it.
Is YCloud free to use?
YCloud has a free tier that includes AI Agent features, an inbox, and basic automation. You only pay when you send outbound messages at scale (WhatsApp's per-conversation charges apply). For a solo founder or small business receiving inbound queries, the free tier is sufficient to start.
Do I need coding skills to build a WhatsApp AI Agent?
No. YCloud's AI Agent is fully no-code. You set up the agent profile, add your business knowledge base (FAQs, product info, pricing), configure assignment rules, and go live — all through a web UI with no programming required.
What is a Meta Business Manager and why do I need it?
Meta Business Manager (business.facebook.com) is Meta's central dashboard for managing your Facebook Page, WhatsApp Business Account, and ad accounts. You need it because the WhatsApp Business API — which powers third-party tools like YCloud — requires a verified business account linked to a Meta Business Manager.
What happens if the AI agent doesn't know the answer?
You configure a human handoff skill in YCloud. When the agent detects it can't answer (or when a user says "talk to a human"), the conversation is transferred to you or another team member. You can set specific trigger phrases like "speak to Prithal" or "need help" to activate the handoff.
Why is my AI agent not responding to messages?
The most common cause is that Priority 2 in Assignment Rules is ON. This routes messages to the last agent who handled the conversation (i.e., you), bypassing the AI. Go to WhatsApp Accounts → Number Settings → Assignment and turn Priority 2 OFF, keeping only Priority 3 (AI Agent) active.
Can I use this for my D2C brand's customer support?
Absolutely — it's one of the best use cases. Train the knowledge base with your product catalogue, shipping policy, return policy, and common FAQs. The AI handles 80–90% of inbound queries automatically, 24/7. You step in only when a customer needs something specific.
How is YCloud different from n8n + Claude for WhatsApp automation?
YCloud is a complete, hosted product — zero setup time, no servers, no coding. n8n + Claude gives you more control and customisation but requires technical setup and a VPS. For most Indian solopreneurs, YCloud is the right starting point. You can graduate to n8n workflows later as needs grow.
